# Stats and trackers

## Stats

Stats (Ctrl 3) has four tabs.

- **Overview**: your streak, focus by day against your goal, what tempted you most, and how time split between productive, neutral, and distracting. With Pause mode on, how often a pause turned you back.
- **Sessions**: every finished session, kept for good. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, or all time, by outcome (kept, let out early, broken), and by profile. Open one to see when it ran, focus against the plan, time away, what was blocked, what happened, and what you wrote: what it was for, your if-then plan, whether it got done (and your 1 to 5), and where you left off. The totals count **Got done** next to Kept: kept is the time, got done is the work. **Export CSV** saves the list to Documents\Sanctum\Exports.
- **Activity**: time by app and by site, for today, 7 days, or 30 days. Change a category with one click, everywhere or only in one profile ("YouTube is productive in Study"). The days already recorded are recounted.
- **Day**: one strip for the day, coloured productive, neutral, or distracting, with each focus session above it. Hover a stretch to see what it was.

Activity comes from the window in front: the app and its title, every 2 seconds, kept on your PC. Raw activity is cleared after 30 days (Setup › Activity), so Activity and Day reach back that far; Sessions keeps everything.

## From your own history

Once there's enough of it, Overview suggests what your sessions show, and only when they clearly show it:

- **The length you finish** ("Your 45-minute seals finish 85% of the time; 90-minute ones 60%."). **Use 45 min** makes it the profile's length. Today shows it by the wheels too when you've picked another.
- **The hours you keep** ("Your kept seals cluster 9 AM to 11 AM."). **Add a focus block** adds a weekday routine there, which Today suggests when it starts.
- **When temptation comes** ("Discord gets in most after 40 minutes."). **Write an if-then** takes you to the plan line.

Fixed rhythms like 25 or 90 minutes don't suit everyone; your own data does better.

## The weekly recap

The first time you open Sanctum each week, a card says last week in a few lines: time in focus and the change from the week before, days kept, the best day, and what tempted you most.

Under it, two optional questions, **What worked?** and **What will you change?**, and **this week's target** in hours. The answers are kept with the week and added to today's journal page when the journal is open. The target starts at your average over the last four weeks; Today's focus strip shows the week against it, and Setup › General changes it. **See the week** opens Stats.

## Trackers

Trackers (Ctrl 4) follow anything else: a number, yes or no, 1 to 10, or a short text. Each has a chart, a table, or both, and an optional goal.

## Check-ins

A check-in asks for a few trackers at a set time, for example a weigh-in at 8:00 AM on Mondays and Thursdays. Log it, snooze it 15 minutes, or skip today. Check-ins never interrupt a seal; they wait until it ends.

Tracker data stays on your PC and is never shown to a partner.
