# Stats and trackers

## Stats

Stats (Ctrl 3) has four tabs.

- **Overview**: your streak, focus by day on a ruler with your daily goal as a dashed line (Week or Month), **What tempted you**, and **Where time went** between productive, neutral, and distracting. With Pause mode on, **Pauses turned back**.
- **Sessions**: every finished session, kept for good. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, or all time, by outcome (kept, let out early, broken), and by profile. Open one to see when it ran, focus against the plan, time away, what was blocked, what happened, and what you wrote: what it was for, your if-then plan, whether it got done (and your 1 to 5), and where you left off. The totals count **Got done** next to Kept: kept is the time, got done is the work. **Export CSV** saves the list to Documents\Sanctum\Exports.
- **Activity**: time by app and by site, for today, 7 days, or 30 days. Change a category with one click, everywhere or only in one profile ("YouTube is productive in Study"). The days already recorded are recounted.
- **Day**: one strip for the day, colored productive, neutral, or distracting, with each focus session above it. Hover a stretch to see what it was.

Activity comes from the window in front: the app and its title, every 2 seconds, kept on your PC. Raw activity is cleared after 30 days (**Setup › Activity › Keep history for**), so Activity and Day reach back that far. Setup › Activity also sorts apps and sites into Productive, Neutral, and Distracting, with a **Block** button on distracting ones. Sessions keep their totals for good, but the detail of what was blocked and what happened goes after 90 days.

## From your own history

Once there's enough of it, Overview suggests what your sessions show, and only when they clearly show it:

- **The length you finish** ("Your 45-minute seals finish 85% of the time; 90-minute ones 60%."). **Use 45 min** makes it the profile's length.
- **The hours you keep** ("Your kept seals cluster 9 AM to 11 AM."). **Add a focus block** adds a weekday routine there, which Today suggests when it starts.
- **When temptation comes** ("Discord gets in most after 40 minutes."). **Write an if-then** takes you to the profile's plan in Setup.

Fixed rhythms like 25 or 90 minutes don't suit everyone; your own data does better.

## The weekly recap

Off to start: turn on **Weekly recap** in **Setup › Goals**. Then the first time you open Sanctum each week, a card says last week in a few lines: time in focus and the change from the week before, days kept, and a strip of bars, one per day, with the best day marked.

Under it, one optional line, **One thing to change this week**. It's kept with the week and added to today's journal page when the journal is open. **See the week** opens Stats.

**Week's focus target**, also in Setup › Goals, starts at your recent average; Stats › Overview shows the week against it.

## Trackers

Trackers (Ctrl 4) follow anything else: a number, yes or no, 1 to 10, or a short text. Each has a chart, a table, or both, and an optional goal; charts mark the days at goal and say how many there were. Make and edit them in **Setup › Trackers**.

## Check-ins

A check-in (Setup › Trackers › Check-ins) asks for a few trackers at a set time, for example a weigh-in at 8:00 AM on Mondays and Thursdays. Log it, snooze it 15 minutes, or skip today. Check-ins never interrupt a seal; they wait until it ends.

Tracker data stays on your PC and is never shown to a partner.
