# Stats and trackers

## Stats

Stats (Ctrl 3) has four tabs.

- **Overview**: your streak, focus by day against your goal, what tempted you most, and how time split between productive, neutral, and distracting.
- **Sessions**: every finished session, kept for good. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, or all time, by outcome (kept, let out early, broken), and by profile. Open one to see when it ran, focus against the plan, time away, what was blocked, and what happened. **Export CSV** saves the list to Documents\Sanctum\Exports.
- **Activity**: time by app and by site, for today, 7 days, or 30 days. Change a category with one click, everywhere or only in one profile ("YouTube is productive in Study"). The days already recorded are recounted.
- **Day**: one strip for the day, coloured productive, neutral, or distracting, with each focus session above it. Hover a stretch to see what it was.

Activity comes from the window in front: the app and its title, every 2 seconds, kept on your PC. Raw activity is cleared after 30 days (Setup › Activity), so Activity and Day reach back that far; Sessions keeps everything.

## The weekly recap

The first time you open Sanctum each week, a card says last week in a few lines: time in focus and the change from the week before, days kept, the best day, and what tempted you most. **See the week** opens Stats.

## Trackers

Trackers (Ctrl 4) follow anything else: a number, yes or no, 1 to 10, or a short text. Each has a chart, a table, or both, and an optional goal.

## Check-ins

A check-in asks for a few trackers at a set time, for example a weigh-in at 8:00 AM on Mondays and Thursdays. Log it, snooze it 15 minutes, or skip today. Check-ins never interrupt a seal; they wait until it ends.

Tracker data stays on your PC and is never shown to a partner.
