# Leaving early

Getting out of a seal early is a climb, not a button. Press **End early** on Today (or Break the seal in the tray) and complete each step in order.

1. **Say why.** Write a reason of at least 50 characters, then wait 5 minutes. The wait pauses while Sanctum isn't in front.
2. **Mean it.** Retype a paragraph of about 300 characters, exactly.
3. **Ask.** With a partner, they get an email and answer from their phone with their PIN. Without one, the seal ends after the paragraph.

## With a partner

- A request lasts 30 minutes. Approved, the session ends early and doesn't count as broken.
- Denied or expired, the seal holds, and you can ask again in 15 minutes, starting from the paragraph.
- If your partner can't be reached because you're offline, a 5-minute wait takes the place of their answer.

## Emergency unlock

One a day, back at midnight. It skips the steps and ends the seal now. It doesn't break your streak, but the day still needs your focus goal. Your partner is told.

## What counts as broken

Sanctum being gone for more than a minute (closed, killed, or the PC off), changing the clock, or turning off the guard or the extension mid-seal breaks it. Your streak resets, and your partner hears about it.
