# Your first seal

On **Today** (Ctrl 1), pick a profile and a length on the wheels, then press **Enter focus** (Ctrl Enter).

## What happens

- The profile's apps and links open.
- Anything on your Distractions list closes as it opens, with a note that sends you back to the work.
- The app turns cobalt. Week, Stats, Trackers, and Setup lock until the seal ends; Today, Notes, and the Journal stay open.
- Windows Do Not Disturb turns on, and your setting comes back after.

## While you work

Press **Ctrl M** for a small always-on-top timer, or use the tray icon. Step away and the countdown pauses, and the end moves later, so the time you get is time you focused.

## When it ends

**Sanctum held** shows what you kept: minutes in focus, attempts blocked, and today against your goal. If the session was linked to a task, it offers to check it off.

To get out before the end, see [Leaving early](/docs/leaving-early).
