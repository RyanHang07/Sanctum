# Your first seal

On **Today** (Ctrl 1), pick a profile and a length on the wheels, then press **Enter focus** (Ctrl Enter).

## Two optional lines

Under the wheels:

- **Will be done**: what this seal is for, in a few words ("Problem set 4"). A specific goal with feedback beats "do your best".
- **If I'm tempted, I'll ___**: what you'll do instead ("stand up and stretch"). Each profile remembers it, and whenever something is caught, Sanctum says it back: "If you're tempted, you said you'd stand up and stretch." Plans in this if-then shape make the plan run when the moment comes.

If you left a note last time on the same task (or in the same profile), it shows above them: "Last time: …".

## What happens

- The profile's apps and links open.
- Anything on your Distractions list closes as it opens, with a note that sends you back to the work.
- The app turns cobalt. Week, Stats, Trackers, and Setup lock until the seal ends; Today, Notes, and the Journal stay open.
- Windows Do Not Disturb turns on, and your setting comes back after.

## While you work

Press **Ctrl M** for a small always-on-top timer, or use the tray icon. Step away and the countdown pauses, and the end moves later, so the time you get is time you focused.

A thought that isn't the work ("email Alex") can wait: press **Ctrl Alt P** in any app, type it, and press Enter. It's parked as a task for today, out of your head. The box under the timer on Today, the tray panel, and the extension's sealed page park too. Ctrl Alt P only works while you're sealed.

## When it ends

**Sanctum held** shows what you kept: minutes in focus, attempts blocked, and today against your goal. If the session was linked to a task, it offers to check it off.

Then, all optional:

- **Did it get done?** Done, Partly, or No, and **Focus, 1 to 5**. Stats › Sessions counts what got done next to what was kept.
- **Where I left off**: a line the next seal on this task shows you.
- What you **parked**: keep it on today's list, move it to tomorrow, or delete it.
- **Take a break**: 5, 10, or 15 minutes with your Distractions list still closed. Today counts it down, and when it ends, "Next block?" seals the same profile and length in one click. Ending a break early is free.

If the seal broke, the page says what happened ("Sanctum was closed 12 minutes in.") and offers the fix, like turning on Protection.

To get out before the end, see [Leaving early](/docs/leaving-early).
