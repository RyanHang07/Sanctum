# Your first seal

On **Today** (Ctrl 1), pick a **Profile** and a **Length** (the arrow keys work too), then press **Enter focus** (Ctrl Enter). The slot at the top of the card shows the focus block that's next, or the task you linked.

## A plan for temptation

In **Setup › Profiles**, each profile has **If I'm tempted, I'll ___**: what you'll do instead ("stand up and stretch"). Set it once, and whenever something is caught, Sanctum says it back: "If you're tempted, you said you'd stand up and stretch." Plans in this if-then shape make the plan run when the moment comes.

If you left a note last time on the same task (or in the same profile), it shows under Enter focus: "Last time: …".

## What happens

- The profile's apps and links open.
- Anything on your Distractions list closes as it opens, with a note that sends you back to the work.
- A thin seal line draws across the card, and the app takes your theme's accent. The card shows when the seal ends, the apps sealed, and the attempts blocked. Week, Stats, Trackers, and Setup lock until the seal ends; Today, Notes, and the Journal stay open.
- Windows Do not disturb turns on, and your setting comes back after. Turn this off in **Setup › Sound and Alerts**.

## While you work

Time left counts down in whole minutes. Press **Compact** (Ctrl M) for a small always-on-top timer, or use the tray icon. Step away and the countdown pauses, and the end moves later, so the time you get is time you focused.

A thought that isn't the work ("email Alex") can wait: press **Ctrl Alt P** in any app, type it, and press Enter. It's parked as a task for today, out of your head. The tray panel and the extension's sealed page park too. Ctrl Alt P only works while you're sealed.

## When it ends

**Sanctum held.** says what the session did in a line. **Done** (Esc) closes it, and **Enter again** (Enter) seals the same profile and length.

**Add a note** opens the rest, all optional:

- If the session was linked to a task, check it off.
- **Did it get done?** Done, Partly, or No, and a focus rating from 1 to 5. Stats › Sessions counts what got done next to what was kept.
- **Where I left off**: a line the next seal on this task shows you.
- What you **parked**: keep it on today's list, move it to tomorrow, or delete it.

If the seal broke, the page says what happened ("Sanctum was closed 12 minutes in.") and offers the fix, like **Turn on Protection**, which opens Setup › App Guard.

To get out before the end, see [Leaving early](/docs/leaving-early).
