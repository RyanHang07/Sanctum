# Adding a partner

A partner holds the last step of [leaving early](/docs/leaving-early): they approve or deny from their phone with a PIN only they know. It's optional, and you can have one partner.

## Invite

1. In **Setup › Partner**, sign in with Google or an email link.
2. Send the invite by email, or copy the link. It works once and lasts 48 hours.
3. Your partner signs in and chooses their PIN. Setup shows when they've accepted.

You can't add or change a partner during a seal.

## Removing a partner

Ask from Setup › Partner. Your partner has to release you, so it can't be used as a way out mid-seal.

## What your account holds

Your email, the partner link, and your requests. Your activity, notes, journal, and stats stay on your PC. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
