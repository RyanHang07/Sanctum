# Profiles and distractions

## Profiles

A profile is a kind of work. Each has:

- **What it opens**: apps and links that start when you enter focus.
- **A default length**, from 15 minutes to 2 hours.
- **Work types**, which suggest what to open.

Edit them in **Setup › Profiles**. A calendar block or routine tagged with a profile suggests it when it starts.

## Distractions

One list decides what every seal blocks, in **Setup › Distractions**:

| Kind | Example | Blocked by |
|---|---|---|
| App | `discord.exe` | Sanctum closes it as it starts |
| Site | `youtube.com` | the extension, and Protection in every browser |
| Link | `reddit.com/r/all` | the extension |
| Keyword | `shorts` | tabs and windows whose title matches |

A site can have **Allow pages**, for example one lecture on `youtube.com`.

Flagged also means counted as distracting time in Stats. Add a distraction any time, even mid-seal. Removing one or allowing pages waits until the seal ends.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours block your Distractions list on a schedule outside sessions. A 15-minute pause is there when you need it, and it asks why. Setup › General can also turn on Windows' Do not disturb for quiet hours.

## Pause mode

A softer guard for the rest of the day. Setup › Distractions › **Pause mode**: off, all day, or set hours. Outside seals, an app or site from your Distractions list asks "Still want it?" before it opens:

- The app is minimized behind the question (a site waits on the extension's page) for five seconds.
- **Not now** (Enter) closes it. **Go on** brings it back and leaves it alone for 15 minutes.
- Seals and quiet hours still block outright. Stats › Overview counts how often a pause turned you back.

A pause of a second or two before opening an app cuts how often it gets opened by more than half.
