# Profiles and distractions

## Profiles

A profile is a kind of work. Each has:

- **What it opens**: apps and links that start when you enter focus.
- **A default length**, from 15 minutes to 2 hours.
- **Work types**, which suggest what to open.

Edit them in **Setup › Profiles**: open one for its name, length, what it opens, and its if-then plan, and the back button returns to the list. A calendar block or routine tagged with a profile suggests it when it starts.

## Distractions

One list decides what every seal blocks, under **What Sanctum blocks** in **Setup › Distractions**:

| Kind | Example | Blocked by |
|---|---|---|
| App | `discord.exe` | Sanctum closes it as it starts |
| Site | `youtube.com` | the extension, and Protection in every browser |
| Link | `reddit.com/r/all` | the extension |
| Keyword | `shorts` | tabs and windows whose title matches |

A site can **Allow a page**, for example one lecture on `youtube.com`.

Under the list, **Suggested** offers sites and apps you spend time on. Add one, or dismiss it; a dismissed suggestion comes back after a few days, or sooner if your time on it jumps.

Flagged also means counted as distracting time in Stats. Add a distraction any time, even mid-seal. Removing one or allowing pages waits until the seal ends.

## Quiet hours

**Quiet hours**, under **When it blocks** in Setup › Distractions, block your Distractions list on a schedule outside sessions. A 15-minute pause is there when you need it, and it asks why. **Setup › Sound and Alerts › Do not disturb in quiet hours** can also turn on Windows' Do not disturb for them.

## Pause mode

A softer guard for the rest of the day. Turn on **Pause before opening** in Setup › Distractions, then pick **All day** or **These hours**. Outside seals, an app or site from your Distractions list makes you wait before it opens ("Discord can wait five seconds."):

- The app is minimized behind the pause (a site waits on the extension's page) for five seconds.
- **Not now** (Enter) closes it. **Go on** brings it back and leaves it alone for 15 minutes.
- Seals and quiet hours still block outright. Stats › Overview counts **Pauses turned back**.

A pause of a second or two before opening an app cuts how often it gets opened by more than half.
