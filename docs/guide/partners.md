# Being a partner

Someone asked you to hold the key: when they want out of a focus session early, you decide. This site is where you answer.

## Accepting

The invite is a link, by email or from them directly. Open it, sign in with Google or an email link (no password), and choose a **PIN of 6 to 12 digits**. Only you know it; it's stored hashed, and nobody can read it.

## Answering a request

When they ask to leave early, you get an email. It opens the request: their reason, the session, the time left, and how often they've asked this week. Enter your PIN and choose **Approve** or **Deny**, with an optional note back.

- Approve, and their session ends early without counting as broken.
- Deny, and the seal holds. They can ask again in 15 minutes.
- A request expires after 30 minutes unanswered, and the seal holds.

Three wrong PINs lock approvals for 30 minutes.

## What you're told

Email only, for: unlock requests, emergency unlocks, broken seals, and a lost streak. Nothing about what they do on their PC, what sites they visit, or their notes.

## Stepping down

On your [partner page](/partner), choose **Step down** at any time. If they ask you to step down, nothing changes until you release them, so removing you can't be used to get out of a seal.
