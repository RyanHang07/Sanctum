# Journal

The Journal (Ctrl 6) is one page per day. It stays on your PC, is never shared or synced, and you can write in it while sealed.

## A page a day

The strip at the top moves between days; a dot marks the days you wrote. The day's card shows what it held: focus, sessions, tasks, counters, and how you felt, with a one-tap mood that logs to a Mood tracker.

## Prompts

The first visit asks what a page should ask: pick prompts by theme (reflection, productivity, gratitude, and more) and how many a day. Each day draws from your picks. Pick none for a blank page. Change them in **Journal settings**.

## The lock

Choose no lock, **Windows Hello** (face, fingerprint, or your Windows PIN), or a journal PIN of 4 to 8 digits. It locks again when you leave the Journal, hide Sanctum, or step away for 5 minutes.

The lock keeps people at this PC out. Pages aren't encrypted on disk.
