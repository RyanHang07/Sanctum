# Journal

The Journal (Ctrl 6) is one page per day. It stays on your PC, is never shared or synced, and you can write in it while sealed.

## The first time

One screen: **Lock Your Journal (Optional)**, then **Start writing**. Change the lock any time in **Journal settings**.

## A page a day

The strip at the top moves between days; each day carries its kept or missed mark, and a dot marks the days you wrote. The day's card shows what it held: focus, sessions, tasks, counters, and a one-tap mood that logs to a Mood tracker.

## Prompts

A new page opens blank, with one prompt above it: "Try: …". **Use it** puts the prompt on the page as a small heading to write under, and **Another** offers the next. A new journal starts from a starter set of prompts; **Change prompts** on the same line picks your own, by theme (productivity, reflection, gratitude, and more) or from quick picks like evening reflection.

## Looking back

Under the day's card, a quiet line shows the page you wrote on this day a month or a year before, when there is one. **Older pages** opens a month calendar to reach any day, and a search across every page.

## Journal settings

One dialog holds the prompts and the lock, saved together.

## The lock

Choose **No lock**, **Windows Hello** (face, fingerprint, or your Windows PIN), or **A journal PIN** of 4 to 8 digits. It locks again when you leave the Journal, hide Sanctum, or step away for 5 minutes.

The lock keeps out people at this PC. Pages aren't encrypted on disk.

## Export

**Setup › Your Data › Export the journal** saves every page as **Markdown** or **JSON**. Unlock the journal first.
