# Notes and sharing

Notes (Ctrl 5) are for everything that isn't a task. They stay open while you're sealed and live on your PC.

## Writing

Type **/** for blocks: headings, lists, checklists, tables, quotes, callouts, toggles, dividers, code, and images. Markdown works as you type: `- `, `1. `, `[] `, `## `, `> `, `---`. Paste or drop an image.

Type **@** to link a task, routine, tracker, or day. **/** then **Make it a task for today** turns a line into a Today task.

Move a block with **Alt ↑ / Alt ↓** or its drag handle.

## Sharing a note

Choose **Share** on a note, then how long: 1 hour, 1 day, or 7 days. Anyone with the link can read it, live, as you edit. Choose **Stop sharing** to end it early.

- **End-to-end encrypted.** The note and its images are encrypted on your PC. The key is the part of the link after `#`, which browsers never send to a server, so Sanctum can't read what you share.
- **Images lose their metadata** (location, camera, times) before they leave your PC.
- **It ends cleanly.** Stopping, deleting the note, or the link expiring removes the shared copy.

Sharing needs a Sanctum account (Setup › Partner).
