# FAQ

## Can't I just close it?

While you're sealed, closing the window sends Sanctum to the tray, and Quit is off. With Protection on, a killed Sanctum is back within seconds, and the seal is logged as tampered.

## What if my PC restarts mid-session?

The seal picks up where it left off, but being gone for more than a minute breaks it. Plan restarts between seals.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Blocking, sessions, streaks, notes, the journal, and trackers all work offline. Calendar sync waits, and a partner request becomes a 5-minute wait.

## Is it really free?

Yes. MIT licensed, with no account required, no ads, and no tracking.

## What does Sanctum know about me?

Everything it records stays in a database on your PC. The optional account holds only what the partner and note sharing need. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. macOS is planned.

## Why does Windows warn about the installer?

Sanctum isn't code-signed yet. See [Install Sanctum](/docs/install).
