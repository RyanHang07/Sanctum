# Today and Week

## Today

Today (Ctrl 1) is the focus row and the day's list together: routines, one-time tasks, and calendar events. Add a task with Enter, check it off, and link it to a session.

**Quick add** understands plain words: `mock interview thu 3pm`, `read 90m @study`, `weigh every mon thu`, `stretch daily`. A repeat makes a routine instead of a one-time item.

## Week

Week (Ctrl 2) shows seven days of tasks and events, with each day's routines folded under a **Routines** row at the top. Click a day's row to open it. Open days stay open as you switch tabs, and going to a different week starts them all closed. Drag items between days. Routines have their own view, and **Month** shows the whole month with each day's progress. Undone items from earlier days wait in **Pending**.

## Google Calendar

Connect in **Setup › Calendar**. Sanctum makes its own **Sanctum** calendar for your timed tasks and the routines you choose, and shows the calendars you pick.

- Timed items sync both ways; untimed ones stay on your PC.
- Routines stay in Sanctum until you turn on **Show on Google Calendar** in one (it needs a time). Then it's one recurring event in Google, and turning it off removes that event.
- Checking something off adds ✓ to the event.
- A block titled `#focus:Deep Work` suggests that profile when it starts.
- A meeting with other people puts Sanctum **In event**, and you can queue focus for when it ends.

Edits made offline wait and sync when you're back.
