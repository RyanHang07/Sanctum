- **Today has a ruler of the day.** Under the headline, a ruler with a tick every 5 minutes runs from 0 to 3 hours (longer when your goal or your day needs it). An accent needle and fill mark the minutes you've focused, the figure beside it reads "42m focused", and what's left to your goal rides between the needle and the goal. The goal is one tall tick in the theme's second colour; the other ticks stay neutral.
- **Focused minutes move while you watch.** During a seal, each minute glides the needle forward and rolls the figure like a counter, and passing a half hour lights its tick for a moment. A reload or a resume jumps straight to the right place instead of replaying. With Windows' animations off, the values just change.
- **Reaching the goal keeps the day, calmly.** The goal tick gains a soft second-colour field and "· kept", the headline changes, today's cell in the streak turns kept, and the streak counts up after it. It plays only when it happens on screen; opening Today later shows the kept day without a replay. No sound, no glow, no confetti.
- **The days strip shows how the week went.** Kept days are the second colour, a missed day is a hollow outline, and today fills with the accent as you focus. The key (Kept, Missed, Today) appears beside the streak only while you hover it or tab to it, and the streak's label reads it all out for screen readers.
- **The streak stays while sealed,** so the day can turn kept in front of you. Search and Customize still step aside.
- **Profile and Length are pickers you can drive from the keyboard.** Up or Down opens the list on your current choice, the arrows, Home and End move, a letter jumps to the next option starting with it, Enter picks, and Escape closes without changing anything. They replace the scroll wheels.
- **Entering focus seals in place.** The Enter focus button's fill collapses into a thin accent line along the bottom of the card, which holds for a moment and then shows the session's progress beneath it, while Profile, Time left and the session's minutes fade into the same slots. It plays however you enter focus (Ctrl Enter, the tray, a scheduled start), and not for a session that was already running.
- **"End early" is quiet.** The sealed card's exit is a small outlined button next to Compact, not a large one. Time left counts down in whole minutes on the odometer.
- **Today's labels are sentence case,** and readable: "Profile", "Length", "Time left", "Discord closes when you enter". The Park field shows the keyboard focus ring around the whole field.
- **Counters roll their totals** on the same odometer, their bar tracks the total without reflowing, and reaching the goal adds a small second-colour goal mark instead of a light sweep.
- **Pending's toggle is easier to read,** in muted text instead of faint.
