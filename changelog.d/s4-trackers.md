- **Tracker charts sit on rulers.** Each chart has a value ruler on the left and a ruler of days along the bottom: one tick a day in 7D and 30D, a week in 90D and a month in 1Y. A day at goal gets a taller tick and a bold date in the theme's second colour, and the goal itself is a short second-colour mark on the left ruler with a dashed line across. The card says how many days were at goal ("5 of 8 days at goal") and what the goal is ("Goal 170 lb or less"). A goal counts downward when the tracker started above it, like a weight.
- **Logging an entry shows it land.** The tracker's figure rolls to the new value, and if the entry reaches the goal, today's tick grows to full height. With Windows' animations off, both just change.
- **The check-in fits a short window.** The dialog is 480 wide and opens higher, and its questions scroll inside it while the title and the Log button stay put; a hairline under the title shows once they've scrolled. The title now leads, with "Scheduled for 9:00 PM" under it.
- **Check-in sparklines show the goal** as a dashed line in the second colour, and the 1 to 10 scale has its sparkline too.
- **Yes or no and 1 to 10 answer like radio buttons:** one tab stop per question, and the arrow keys move the answer. Picking the chosen answer again still leaves the question blank.
- **The range and view switches are the shared segmented control,** and Trackers' controls use the app's corner radii.
- **Labels in Setup › Trackers are sentence case and readable,** and the hints, the chevrons and the delete button on an entry are no longer drawn in faint text. Text fields there and in the check-in show the app's one focus ring.
