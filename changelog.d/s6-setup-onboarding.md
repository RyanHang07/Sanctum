- **Appearance shows each theme's two colours.** Every theme in the list carries a small pair, its accent beside its second colour, drawn in that theme's own colours. Hovering or tabbing to a theme shows its preview with the pair again, named "Accent" and "Second colour" with their values, and whether it is light or dark.
- **Appearance opens on your theme.** The list scrolls so the theme you're using sits in the middle, instead of starting at the top. Picking another theme leaves the list where it is.
- **The theme lists are tidy.** Catppuccin Latte is in the light list, and the two old "Light" Catppuccin copies are gone. If you had picked one of them, Latte shows as your choice.
- **General shows the goal tick.** The daily focus goal has the same tall second-colour tick as Today's ruler beside it. It is a solid mark, with no glow.
- **Every onboarding step has a one-sentence headline,** from the same source as the rest of the app: "Define Interview Prep.", "Pick a look you can change any time.", "You're set." and so on. Each new step rises in a few pixels and fades in; with Windows' animations off it just appears.
- **Onboarding's profile switch is the shared segmented control,** with arrow keys and a panel. Ticked work shows its check in the right colour, and unticked boxes have an edge you can see in every theme.
- **The check-in time picker follows the theme.** In Setup › Trackers, light themes get a light time picker instead of a dark one.
- **Setup's labels are sentence case and readable.** The nav groups (Focus, Tracking, Connections, App), "Show in Sanctum", the app picker's groups and the quiet hours labels drop their capitals, and hints, counts, the add-field icons, the ↵ hints and the "not installed" notes move from faint to muted text.
- **Every Setup field shows the app's one focus ring.** The Distractions add field draws it around the whole field.
- **Sounds' styles are the shared segmented control,** and picking one still plays it.
- **Connected is a neutral dot.** Calendar and Browser show a muted dot when connected and a hollow ring when not, instead of the event colour.
- **Profiles read more cleanly.** An app or site row in a profile highlights while you hover or tab into it, and its remove button shows for both. Seals counts say "1 app" and "1 keyword", not "1 apps". Switching Setup sections no longer fades the page in.
