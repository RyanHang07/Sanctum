- **Stats draws the week on a ruler.** The day chart has a thin tick axis on its left (hours in the theme's second colour, quarter hours in the line colour) and the daily goal is a dashed second-colour line named "2h a day". There's no best-day mark, and no glow or backdrop.
- **Bars are what you focused, and kept days are marked.** Bars stay the accent and the legend calls them "Focused". A kept day gets a short second-colour mark on its bar's cap, like Week's kept dot. "Goal missed" is now "Missed", and missed bars have an edge you can see in every theme. In Month, kept days get the same second-colour dot.
- **The week's focus sits on its own ruler.** The summary row shows the week's focused time against this week's target ("focused of 10h. 2h to go.") on an hour scale, with the fill in the accent and the target in the second colour. The figures roll like a counter when they change.
- **Day details work from the keyboard.** Every day in the chart and the month grid can be focused. Hovering or focusing a day shows its details in one fixed spot at the end of the legend row, read out by screen readers. Clicking a day opens it in the Day tab.
- **The bars move when the figures do.** Stepping to another week or the minute refresh glides each bar from its old height, and a day that turns kept while you watch fades its mark in. With Windows' animations off, they just change.
- **Stats' tabs are a real tablist.** Overview, Sessions, Activity and Day are the shared segmented control, with arrow keys and a panel, and switch without a fade. The Week and Month range and the Sessions and Activity ranges are pressed-button groups.
- **Small text in Stats reads in every theme.** Tempted kinds, percentages, planned minutes, the Day timeline's hours and the month grid's weekday names move from faint to muted text.
